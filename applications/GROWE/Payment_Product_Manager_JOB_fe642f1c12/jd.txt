Payment Product Manager at GROWE
Location: Fully Remote / Worldwide (Anywhere, no country restrictions)
About GROWE:
GROWE is a global technology and business advisory group managing entertainment, transactional, and high-frequency payment systems worldwide.
Key Responsibilities:
- Define and standardize payment hub system behaviors: gateway configurations, withdrawal hold/release logic, callback timeouts, and customer error messaging.
- Own and optimize seven core back-office operational screens: projects, gateways, payment groups, methods, orders, transactions, and logs.
- Lead internal discovery across payment rails: wallets, KYC verification, anti-fraud checks, reconciliation, and ledger accuracy.
- Manage rollout stages, sequencing traffic percentage migrations, transaction replays, and rollback mechanisms.
- Monitor integration health, incident management, and maintain deep observability into payment success rates and gateway approval percentages.
Requirements:
- 4+ years of product management experience, with at least 2 years explicitly owning payments, fintech, or complex transactional systems.
- Deep technical competence: ability to read REST API contracts, inspect webhooks, and trace individual transactions across distributed microservices.
- Data fluency: strong command of conversion funnels, authorization rates, gateway drop-off analysis, and SQL.
- Strong stakeholder management across compliance, engineering, and customer support.
