Product Manager at Globalfy
Location: 100% Remote Worldwide (Global Remote)

Globalfy is an all-in-one platform helping global entrepreneurs incorporate businesses, open business bank accounts, and manage international financial compliance.

- Drive product discovery by analyzing customer feedback, support tickets, and quantitative funnel data in Mixpanel.
- Formulate PRDs, technical user stories, and acceptance criteria in Notion and Jira, guiding engineering squads through Agile sprints.
- Oversee operational release management, coordinating QA staging sign-offs, production rollouts, changelog publishing, and internal training.
- Partner with compliance and finance to ensure workflows comply with banking regulations, KYC/KYB standards, and tax filings.
Requirements:
- 3+ years of total software product management experience, with at least 2 years owning product initiatives in B2B SaaS or FinTech.
- Proven experience shipping customer-facing features with measurable business impact (retention uplift, churn reduction, conversion gains).
- Strong technical fluency: understanding APIs, webhooks, database queries, and release cycles.
- Experience with product analytics (Mixpanel) and agile project management tools (Jira).
